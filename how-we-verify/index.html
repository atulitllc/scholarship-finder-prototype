How we verify

Last checked Oct 12, 2026 · Sample data

  1. Source. We only list scholarships with an official sponsor page.
  2. Check. Amount, awards, eligibility, and deadline with time zone are confirmed weekly.
  3. Exclude. No application fees, sweepstakes, "no essay" lead gen, or listings that sell your data.
  4. Label. Sponsored listings are marked and held to the same rules. Ads never appear inside data tables and we never run loan ads.
  5. Expire. Passed deadlines are marked with the expected next cycle.
